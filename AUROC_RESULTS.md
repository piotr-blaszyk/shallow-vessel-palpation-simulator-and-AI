# AUROC across the six canonical scenarios

Per-node vessel-classification AUROC for each (train → test) configuration,
scored twice: once from the **published** Zenodo checkpoint and once from a
checkpoint **retrained** locally with `--train`.

Datasets: **A** = simulation, **B** = real silicone phantom, **C** = real meat phantom.

**Granularity: per-marker-node, not reprojected.** Each score compares the
model's per-node probability against the per-node ground-truth label, for every
ViTacTip marker in every evaluated video frame, pooled across all clips. This is
deliberately *not* the binned 2D→3D→2D reprojection map used for the
vessel-localisation results — that aggregation exists only for the silicone
phantom and cannot be built for meat at all. The node counts below
(clips × 127 markers) confirm the granularity: 12700 = 100 silicone clips × 127,
25273 = 199 meat clips × 127.

Generated by:

```bash
python -m difftactile.scripts.script_segmentation_gnn <config> --train   # for the retrained rows
python -m difftactile.scripts.script_auroc_all_scenarios                 # scores all six
```

ROC curves (one PDF per scenario, named after it):
`difftactile/output/roc_curves/roc_curve_<config>_<weights>.pdf`

runs and are expected to differ (training is stochastic).

## Reading the numbers

- **A-to-B (0.7314 vs 0.72)** and **C-to-B (0.6786 vs 0.68)** reproduce the paper
  to rounding. These two are deterministic evaluation paths, so the agreement is
  exact rather than approximate.

- **A-to-C (0.8183 vs 0.60) does not match, and the repository is the correct
  side of the disagreement.** The paper's A→C figures were produced by an
  evaluation path that fed the checkpoint **unnormalised** inputs: the
  `test_dataset.set_stats(stats)` call sat inside a dead `if False:` block on the
  old `sim-to-meat-test` branch, so the dataset's `warmup` flag stayed set and
  normalisation was a silent no-op — even though the model had been trained on
  normalised inputs. Applying the statistics the checkpoint expects raises the
  cross-domain vein IoU from 0.034 to 0.198 (background 0.888 → 0.809), and the
  AUROC from ~0.60 to 0.82. This was found and fixed before this measurement run;
  see `REPRODUCTION_TEST.md`, which already flags that "if 0.034 (or anything
  derived from it) appears in the paper, that figure needs revisiting". **It
  does** — both Table `tab:iou`'s sim→meat row (FG 0.03) and Fig.
  `roc-curve`(c) (AUROC 0.60) derive from the unnormalised path.

  The practical consequence for the manuscript is that the reported degradation
  under transfer to the raw-meat phantom is **overstated**. The corrected numbers
  still show a real domain gap in per-node IoU (0.198 vs 0.231 on silicone), but
  the AUROC ordering changes: A→C is no longer the worst configuration.

- **Retraining tracks the published checkpoints.** A-to-B and A-to-C come out
  slightly above their published counterparts and C-to-B essentially on top of
  its own (0.6737 vs 0.6786), so the published checkpoints are representative
  rather than lucky runs.

- **Class imbalance** is severe (7–11% positive), which is why the paper treats
  per-node IoU as a diagnostic and reports AUROC alongside it.
